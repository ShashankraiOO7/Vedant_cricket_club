FAQ

Common questions

What are the academy timings?

Morning sessions run from 5:30 AM to 8:00 AM and evening sessions from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM.

How can I register for a tournament?

You can use the tournament registration form on the Tournaments page and submit the required details.

How should I contact the club quickly?

The fastest methods are direct phone call and WhatsApp using the contact details on this page.

Do I need documents for admission?

Yes, basic student details and any requested supporting documents should be kept ready before submission.

Can I ask questions before filling the form?

Yes, you can contact the club first on call, WhatsApp, or email for any admission or tournament-related help.

Is tournament registration confirmed immediately?

No, registration is reviewed first and confirmation depends on complete details and event availability.
