# Privacy Policy - Vedant Cricket Club

Effective Date: March 17, 2026

Vedant Cricket Club is committed to protecting the privacy of players, parents, teams, and website visitors.

## 1. Information We Collect
- Name, phone number, and contact details.
- Academy admission and tournament registration data.
- Documents voluntarily uploaded by users.
- Basic technical data for security and analytics.

## 2. Purpose of Data Collection
- To process admissions, registrations, and enquiries.
- To share important updates related to training and events.
- To maintain fair participation and identity verification.
- To improve website security and user experience.

## 3. Data Sharing
- We do not sell personal information.
- Data may be shared only with authorized internal staff or service providers needed for operations.
- Data may be disclosed if legally required by applicable law.

## 4. Data Retention
- Personal information is kept only for operational, legal, or administrative necessity.
- Unused records are periodically reviewed and removed where appropriate.

## 5. User Rights
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of data, subject to legal or record-keeping requirements.
- Ask for details about what data is stored.
