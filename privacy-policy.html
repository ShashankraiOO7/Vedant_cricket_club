Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 18, 2026

Your information and how we use it

Vedant Cricket Club uses submitted data only for academy admissions, tournament registrations, enquiries, communication, and website operations.

Data Collected

  • Name, mobile number, WhatsApp number, and contact information
  • Student, guardian, player, or team details submitted through forms
  • Uploaded files such as ID, photo, or team list
  • Basic technical and usage information for security and performance

Primary Purpose

  • Admission, registration, and enquiry processing
  • Fixture, batch, and notice communication
  • Verification, coordination, and records management
  • Site improvement and operational security

Data Protection

  • Information is handled only for internal operational use
  • We do not sell personal data to outside parties
  • Access is limited to authorized team members when required
  • Records may be retained for administrative or legal needs

Your Rights

  • You may request correction of inaccurate submitted data
  • You may ask for deletion of information, subject to operational requirements
  • You may contact the club for privacy-related support

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as processes, website functionality, or legal obligations change. The latest version will remain available on this page.
